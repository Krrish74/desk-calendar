Desk Calendar

Desk Calendar is a personal calendar app for one person's own Google account. It shows your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks on your phone and desktop, lets you add and change them (including with a short quick-add line like "gym tomorrow 6pm"), sends reminder notifications before events, and has an optional AI assistant that can answer questions about your schedule and plan your tasks into time blocks.

It is a private app: only the Google account its owner has allowed can sign in. Anyone else is turned away, even after signing in with Google.

What it uses your Google data for

  • Google Calendar: to show your calendars and events, and to create, edit, move and delete events when you ask it to.
  • Google Tasks: to show your tasks and to add, complete, edit and delete them when you ask it to.
  • Your email address: only to check that you are the allowed account.

Details are in the privacy policy.
