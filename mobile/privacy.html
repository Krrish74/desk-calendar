Privacy policy

Desk Calendar · Last updated November 22, 2026

Desk Calendar is a personal app run by its owner for their own Google account. Only the Google account(s) on the owner's allow-list can sign in; nobody else's data is accessed or stored. This page explains what the app does with your data.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events): your calendar list and events, to show them and to create, change, move and delete events when you ask the app or its assistant to.
  • Google Tasks (tasks): your task lists and tasks, to show them and to add, complete, change and delete tasks when you ask.
  • Your email address (openid, email): only to check that you are an allowed account.

What is stored, and where

  • The app runs on Cloudflare Workers. Its database (Cloudflare D1) stores your Google sign-in tokens, encrypted; sign-in sessions for your devices; your app settings (including the optional "About you" working-hours profile); the current assistant conversation; a running total of assistant usage and cost; and, if you turn on reminders, each device's push-notification address.
  • Your calendar events and tasks themselves stay in your Google account. The app reads them when it needs them and does not keep its own copy on the server, apart from what appears in the current assistant conversation.
  • On your phone, the app keeps your most recently loaded events and tasks so it can open without a connection. This copy is deleted when you sign out.
  • The app uses one cookie, to keep you signed in. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who else sees data

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its database.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): only if you use the assistant. Your message, and the calendar and task details the assistant looks up to answer it, are sent to Anthropic's API to produce the reply, under Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Your phone's push service (for iPhone, Apple): only if you turn on reminders. Each reminder is encrypted so that only your device can read it.

Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Desk Calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is not used to develop, improve or train AI models, and is not transferred except as needed to provide those features (as described under "Who else sees data").

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Sign-in sessions last up to 180 days and renew while you use the app.
  • The assistant conversation is replaced by a new one after 30 idle minutes or 20 messages; you can also start a new one at any time.
  • Records of reminders already sent are deleted after two days.
  • Settings → Sign out everywhere revokes the app's access to your Google account and deletes the stored tokens, all sessions, the assistant conversation and all reminder subscriptions. Your app settings remain until the owner deletes the app's database.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
